## What the video shows

A 3–5 minute walkthrough of **CyberRisk – FAIR-Based Cyber Risk Quantification & Investment Optimization Platform**, covering:

1. **The problem (≈30 sec):** Enterprises rate cyber risk as "Low / Medium / High" — labels that don't tell a CISO or board how much money is actually at stake, or whether current security spend is going to the right place.
2. **The proposed solution (≈30 sec):** A FAIR-based Monte Carlo pipeline that converts vulnerability, threat, and control telemetry into Expected Annual Loss (EAL) and Value at Risk (VaR), then mathematically optimizes security investment under a budget using Integer Linear Programming (ILP).
3. **Main features / workflow (≈90 sec):**
   - Dataset upload (drag-and-drop 5 CSVs, ZIP, or JSON) with live schema validation, or one-click demo dataset
   - Executive dashboard: organization-wide EAL/VaR, risk by business unit, controls ranked by ROSI
   - Asset Portfolio drill-down with the Loss Exceedance Curve for a selected asset
   - Investment Optimizer: budget slider, live ILP-vs-greedy comparison, and the resulting recommended action plan
   - AI Risk Copilot answering a live natural-language question about current risk posture
4. **Actual working prototype (≈60–90 sec):** Screen-recorded walkthrough of the live application (not slides) — upload or demo dataset → dashboard loads → run the optimizer at a chosen budget → open the chatbot and ask a real question.
5. **Hardware demonstration:** Not applicable — this is a software-only platform.
